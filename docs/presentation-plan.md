7. Camera and scene interactions with screenshots — Turar, 45 seconds.
8. Tests, hardware, FPS and effect tradeoffs — Nursalim, 60 seconds.

10. Conclusions, limitations and short live demonstration — all, 60 seconds.

Total target: about 9 minutes 20 seconds. Use screenshots from the submitted build and equations actually used. Rehearse questions about matrix order, index buffers, derivatives, normal matrices, lighting coordinate spaces and frame timing.
