This is a plan for the integrated project; no new final video is claimed as recorded.

Record 1920×1080 real-time screen capture, 3–6 minutes. Keep the application's live FPS visible throughout; do not replace it with a fixed number. Voiceover or captions name the feature being demonstrated. VS Code may be visible behind the application, but make the scene and text legible.

2. 0:35–1:20 — identify at least five distinct scene objects; orbit with keyboard and mouse for R2/R6.
3. 1:20–2:00 — steer the car and rotate wheels; explain parent × local child transform for R3.
4. 2:00–2:45 — show two lights and toggle/move one; describe ambient, diffuse and specular for R4.
5. 2:45–3:25 — demonstrate procedural road, speed, pause and palette for R5/R6.
6. 3:25–4:10 — show normal FPS at the stated resolution/hardware, resize and reset; identify R7 evidence. Report the TA-machine test separately.
7. 4:10–4:50 — demonstrate bloom and CRT independently only if implemented; otherwise omit without claiming stretch points.
8. 4:50–5:15 — summarize limitations and source/report locations.

Before recording, build the reviewed version and test every planned control. Record real actions. Trim dead time and unwanted audio without changing the apparent feature behavior or FPS. Watch the exported file, verify duration/resolution/audio, then have a human include the file or accessible link in the final submission.
